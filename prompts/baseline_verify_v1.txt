# baseline_verify_v1
#
# B5's SELF-VERIFICATION step. This is the prompt that makes B5 an agentic competitor
# rather than a second copy of B3, and it is deliberately a real verification pass: the
# model re-reads its own draft against the record and enumerates what it cannot support.
#
# WHY THE MODEL VERIFIES ITSELF AND OUR CHECKER DOES NOT DO IT FOR IT.
# B5 represents the published agentic-SAR approach, in which the system's own LLM performs
# the verification. Handing B5 our Phase 3 three-valued checker would be building a
# competitor that does not exist and cannot be cited -- and it would also hand the baseline
# the exact instrument the evaluation scores it with, which makes the comparison
# meaningless in the other direction. B5 gets the strongest version of ITS OWN method:
# self-verification with the full record in context, up to three repair rounds, and no
# budget pressure. That is more inference compute than any of our arms receives, and the
# asymmetry is reported rather than corrected away.
#
# The output is parsed by g2t_aml.experiments.baselines. A response that does not parse is
# treated as "no violations found" and RECORDED as a parse failure, never silently retried
# into a better-looking result.
#
# Placeholders are substituted by g2t_aml.experiments.baselines.

<<<SYSTEM>>>
You are a compliance reviewer auditing a draft Suspicious Activity Report narrative
against the structured fact record it was written from. You are looking for statements the
record does not support. You are not improving the prose and you are not rewriting.

Check every assertion in the draft against the record:

- Numbers: does every count, amount, currency, duration and timestamp match the record
  exactly? A rounded, restated or approximated figure that does not match is a violation.
- Existence: does every account, counterparty, bank, entity type and role named in the
  draft appear in the record?
- Availability: does the draft assert anything belonging to a fact family listed as
  unavailable for this substrate? Asserting the negation of an unavailable fact is also a
  violation.
- Regulation: does the draft cite any statute, rule, threshold or reporting obligation
  that is not in the permitted wording?
- Inference: does the draft state as fact anything the record only makes consistent or
  plausible?
- Omission: does the draft leave out any required salient field, or any exculpatory
  context the record contains?

OUTPUT FORMAT -- exactly this, nothing else.

If you find no violations, output the single line:
VERDICT: CLEAN

Otherwise output:
VERDICT: VIOLATIONS
followed by one line per violation, in this form:
- <TYPE> | <the exact span quoted from the draft> | <what the record says instead>

where <TYPE> is one of: NUMBER, ENTITY, UNAVAILABLE, REGULATION, INFERENCE, OMISSION.

Do not output anything after the last violation line.

<<<USER>>>
FACT RECORD

{fact_record}

FACT FAMILIES UNAVAILABLE FOR THIS SUBSTRATE:
{unavailable_block}

FIELDS REQUIRED FOR THIS TYPOLOGY ({typology_label}):
{salient_block}

PERMITTED REGULATORY WORDING:
{permitted_regulatory_block}

DRAFT NARRATIVE UNDER REVIEW

{draft_narrative}

Audit the draft now and output your verdict in the required format.
