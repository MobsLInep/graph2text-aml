# baseline_repair_v1
#
# B5's REPAIR step: the draft plus the violations its own verification pass found.
#
# The instruction NOT to delete the offending sentence is the same one silver_repair_v1
# carries, and for the same reason: past a couple of rounds a model stops repairing and
# starts contorting, satisfying the reviewer by deleting whatever the finding was attached
# to. A narrative that passes verification by saying less is not a repaired narrative, and
# a baseline that wins by producing three sentences is a baseline that was measured wrong.
# The coverage requirement below is what stops that, and it is the same requirement our
# own inference guard enforces through its coverage term (D-073).
#
# Placeholders are substituted by g2t_aml.experiments.baselines.

Fix exactly what the review found and change nothing else.

RULES

1. Correct each cited span so that it matches the fact record, or remove the unsupported
   claim from it while keeping the surrounding statement intact.
2. DO NOT delete whole sentences or sections to make a finding go away. A narrative that
   passes review by omitting the finding is a worse filing than the one you started with.
3. The revised narrative must still cover every required salient field, and must still
   include any exculpatory context the record carries.
4. Keep the length within the stated band. Do not shorten the narrative to reduce risk.
5. Output the revised narrative only: no preamble, no explanation of what you changed, no
   commentary.
